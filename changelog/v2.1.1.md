# 2.1.1 — 2026-08-20

Minecraft 1.21.1 | Fabric, NeoForge

## Performance

|         Tag | Change |
|------------:|--------|
| ![improvement] | `ListView` now viewport-culls its children — only elements whose bounds intersect the visible scroll window run their render passes. List draw cost is now bounded by the viewport instead of the total item count, fixing severe frame-rate drops with large lists (e.g. `ItemSelectionView`): a ~10k-item list went from ~11 FPS to a steady 120 FPS. |

## API

|         Tag | Change |
|------------:|--------|
| ![feature] | New protected `GuiElement.shouldRenderChild(GuiElement)` hook, consulted by all three render passes before recursing into a child. Defaults to `true` (no behavior change); containers override it to skip off-screen children. Additive and non-breaking. |

## Internal

|         Tag | Change |
|------------:|--------|
| ![feature] | Added dev-only `ItemPerfTestScreen` GUI performance harness (`/modutilities openItemPerfScreen`), measuring the item list's background/foreground render time and FPS with lowpass-smoothed averages, plus reinsert/clear controls to scale the list. |
