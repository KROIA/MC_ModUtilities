# 2.2.2 — In Development

Minecraft 1.21.11 | Fabric, NeoForge

## API

| Tag | Change |
|---:|---|
| ![feature] | `GenericRequest.sendRequestToMaster(UUID playerUUID, input)` (and the `RequestManager` counterpart): server code on a slave can send a request to the master on behalf of a player, who may be offline. The master's `handleOnMasterServer` receives the UUID as `playerSender`; the response completes the future on the slave and is not relayed to the player |
| ![feature] | `GuiElement.isChildRendered(child)`: public read-only view of `shouldRenderChild`, e.g. whether a list row lies inside the list's scroll window |
| ![feature] | `Label.setEllipsisEnabled(boolean)` (and the `Button` pass-through): overflowing text ends with "..." instead of scrolling back and forth (default off). `Label.getDisplayedText()` returns the text as drawn; `getText()` stays the full text |

## UI/UX

| Tag | Change |
|---:|---|
| ![bugfix] | Drop-down menus open and close on a click anywhere on their label, not only on the small arrow |
| ![bugfix] | Check boxes toggle on a click on their text too, not only on the small box |
